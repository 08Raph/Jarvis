
## Everyday fixes and voice upgrades
- Jarvis opens apps, folders, Settings pages and files without asking (Safety page: you can switch asking back on). Installers, terminals and protected programs are still refused.
- Fixed repeated replies (repeat guard and repeat penalty), wrong time/date (the clock is given to the model and plain time questions are answered directly), and spoken "roleplay," style labels (labels are text only).
- Voice: greeting after the wake phrase (editable), several wake phrases, saying "stop" or talking over Jarvis, a pause of up to 5 seconds before he answers (adjustable), voice changes apply at once.
- Chats are saved automatically and can be reopened; memory learns "my name is..." style facts and accepts typed notes; both stay on this PC.
- Screen helper (off switch on the Safety page): reads highlighted text or the front window, and moves a window to another monitor ("move Discord to monitor 2").
- Closing the window can keep Jarvis running in the tray (default) or quit completely; optional wallpaper display. Nothing here has been run on a physical PC yet; interrupting by voice works best with headphones.
