# Learning notice (training use of your conversations)

Learning is OFF unless you confirm this notice. Read it, then decide.

If you turn learning ON:
- Your messages and the AI's replies sent AFTER you turn it on may be used to train future Project JARVIS models, offline and in batches. Models do not change while you chat.
- Obvious personal data (email addresses, phone-like numbers, links, long secrets, file paths) is automatically removed first. This is best effort and will miss some personal data. Do not type secrets into a chat you let the service learn from.
- Your username is not included. Conversations are not sold or used for ads.
- This is separate from the local personal memory on your own computer, which is never sent anywhere.

Your controls, any time:
- `jarvis online learning off` stops future use and withdraws earlier messages not yet used in a training batch.
- `jarvis online delete-chats` deletes your stored chat history. `jarvis online delete-account` deletes your account.
- Limit: text already used in a training batch cannot be removed from a model that was already trained.

If you do nothing, or decline, no message of yours is used for training.
